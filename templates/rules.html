The Goal:

Your objective is to guess the song that is playing from four options before the time runs out. The timer starts the moment you first play the audio for each round.

Gameplay:

A correct answer increases your score and immediately starts the next round with a new song. A single wrong answer, or running out of time, ends the game. The same song will never be the correct answer twice in one match.

Scoring:

The game tracks your current score during the match. Your personal best for each game mode is saved in your browser, so you can always come back and try to beat your own top score.

Singleplayer:

The fastest way to play. Jump right into the action with our default curated playlist and a fixed timer. It’s perfect for a quick game and for setting new records.

Custom Mode:

This mode gives you control. Use any public playlist or album link from Spotify or Deezer and set your own time limit per round. For this to work, your playlist must be public and contain at least 5 playable tracks.

A Quick Tip for Spotify Playlists: For the best results, we recommend using playlists created by users (like you or your friends). Some official playlists curated by Spotify can have restrictions that prevent third-party games like this one from accessing their tracks. Public playlists made by users almost always work perfectly!
